Current Projects

Organic Sulfur Photochemistry Formation of COS and CS2 in natural waters.

This NSF-funded project evaluates how different indirect photochemical pathways influence COS and CS2 formation in ocean and freshwaters during sunlight photolysis.

publication funded collaboration
Resource Recovery in Critical Materials Critical Materials Recovery from Landfill Leachate

This NSF-funded interdisciplinary project examines the potential for landfill leachate to serve as a source of critical materials by examining fundamental chemical, biological, and physical processes governing its potential recovery.
